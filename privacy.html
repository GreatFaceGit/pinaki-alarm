Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Pinaki Alarm ("we", "our", "the app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. Pinaki Alarm is available on Android and iOS; some features described below (such as advertising) may not be present on every version.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address, used to create and sign in to your account.
  • Account identifier: an internal user ID that links your notifications and settings to your account.
  • Notification data: the times you set, whether they're set against local or UTC time, and any short note you add to a notification.
  • Advertising and analytics data (Android only): on Android, the app uses Google's AdMob to show optional rewarded ads. AdMob may collect a device advertising identifier, approximate (coarse) location derived from IP address, and data about ad interactions and app usage, for the purposes of serving and measuring ads. See Google's own privacy practices at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Diagnostic data: basic crash and performance data, used to keep the app stable and fix bugs.

How We Use Information

  • To operate core app functionality — signing you in, saving and delivering your notifications.
  • To display and measure advertising (Android only), where applicable.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app performance and stability.

Third-Party Services

We use Supabase to store account and notification data on our behalf. On Android, we use Google AdMob to serve optional rewarded ads. These providers process data under their own privacy and security practices.

Data Retention

We retain your account and notification data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time — see below.

Deleting Your Account

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time:

  • In the app: go to Account → Delete Account.
  • On the web: visit pinakialarm.vercel.app/delete-account.html.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Children's Privacy

Pinaki Alarm is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
