Video Ludo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Video Ludo ("the app", "we", "us") is developed by an independent solo developer. This policy explains what happens with your data when you use the app.

What this version of the app does

This release of Video Ludo lets you play Ludo against computer-controlled opponents on your own device. There is no sign-in, no user account, and no connection to the internet required to play.

Data we collect

This version of the app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in is required or offered.
  • No personal information (name, email, phone number, photos) is requested or collected.
  • No gameplay data is sent to any server — your game state exists only on your device for the current session and is not saved once the app closes.
  • No advertising or analytics SDKs are included in this version.
  • No location data is accessed.

Permissions

This version of the app does not request any device permissions (camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or otherwise).

Children's privacy

This version of the app contains no chat, video, account creation, or any feature that collects information from anyone, including children. It is a simple offline dice-and-board game.

Changes coming in future versions

Video Ludo is under active development. Planned future versions will introduce optional accounts, multiplayer matchmaking, and live video/voice chat between players. Before any version that adds these features is published, this privacy policy will be updated in advance to clearly explain what new data is collected (such as account details, matchmaking data, and video/audio during a match), how it is used, and what controls you have — including reporting, blocking, and data deletion. That update will happen before such a version reaches the public, not after.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
