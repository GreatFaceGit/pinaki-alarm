Video Ludo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Video Ludo ("the app", "we", "us") is developed by an independent solo developer. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and what control you have over it.

Who can use the app

The first thing the app asks for is your date of birth. Players under 13 cannot use the app and no account is created for them. Players aged 13 to 17 can play, but video chat, voice chat and profile pictures are only available to players aged 18 and over. Your date of birth is stored on our servers, is never shown to other players, and is used only for these age checks. The app cannot verify it, so it relies on the date you enter.

Accounts

  • Guest play creates an anonymous account with no name or email. If you uninstall the app or change phones, a guest account cannot be recovered.
  • Google sign-in creates a persistent account. We receive your email address and an account identifier from Google. We never receive your Google password.
  • A guest account can be upgraded to a Google account at any time and keeps its coins, gold bars and stats.

Your profile, and what other players can see

Your username, chosen avatar, coin balance, game statistics and (if you add them) profile pictures are visible to other players. Players aged 18 and over may add a profile picture and up to 8 more pictures; anyone who opens your profile can see them. You can remove any picture at any time. Please only add pictures you are happy for other players to see. If a picture is reported, our team may review it and remove it.

Games

Games are run on our servers, so that dice rolls, moves, buy-ins and winnings cannot be altered by a modified copy of the app. We store the state of each game while it is played, the players in it, and its result. Some opponents in a game may be computer-controlled.

Live video and voice chat

Video and voice chat are for players aged 18 and over. When you use them, your audio and video are sent in real time to the other people in that room or game, using LiveKit, a third-party real-time communication provider, to relay the connection. This happens live. We do not record it and we do not store it. Sound is free; video uses gold bars (with a short free allowance per game), and gold bar balances and video time are tracked on our servers.

Reporting and blocking

If you report a player, we save the report: who made it, who it is about, when, the description the reporter wrote, and a picture of the game board at that moment. If the reported player's camera is on at that moment, our server also takes a single still image from it and saves it with the report. Reports about a profile picture include the picture. We keep reports and their pictures only as long as needed to review and act on them, and only our moderator can see them.

If you block a player, you no longer see or hear them and you will not be placed in the same game as them again. The block is saved to your account.

Friends

If you add another player as a friend, your username, avatar and coin balance are visible to them, and they can see whether you are online. You can search for other players by username.

Notifications

With your permission the app shows notifications. Reminders that your free spin or free coins are ready are scheduled on your own phone. Notifications about friend requests, game invitations and voice-room invitations are sent through Expo's push notification service, using a token that identifies your phone; we store that token with your account so we can send them.

Purchases

In-app purchases of coins and gold bars are handled entirely by Google Play's billing system. We never see or store your payment card details. Our servers receive only a purchase confirmation token from Google, which we use to credit your account once.

Advertising

The app shows advertisements through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads. To serve ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier and general device information. We do not receive this information and cannot identify you from it. We request non-personalised ads where possible. You can reset or delete your advertising ID from your device settings.

What we do not collect

  • Your precise location
  • Photos from your gallery, except pictures you choose to upload
  • Text message content (the app has no text chat)
  • Your payment card or bank details

Children's privacy

The app is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly create accounts for them. Players aged 13 to 17 cannot use video, voice or profile pictures.

Data deletion

You can ask us to delete your account and everything linked to it at any time. See how to request deletion. Guest accounts can simply be abandoned by uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
