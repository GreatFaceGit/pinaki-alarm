Roll Adda — Privacy Policy

App: Roll Adda: Video Board Games
Package: com.videoludo.game
Developer: GreatFace
Last updated: 18 October 2026

Roll Adda is a board games app with live video and voice chat. This page explains what it holds about you, why, who else sees it, and how to get rid of it. It is written to be read rather than to be survived.

The app was previously called Video Ludo, which is why its package name still reads com.videoludo.game. It is the same app and the same developer; only the name on the store has changed.

Who you are dealing with

Roll Adda is made by an independent developer, GreatFace, who is the data controller for everything described here. There is no company behind it and no staff. You can reach that person at lakshaykumaar@gmail.com.

Age

Roll Adda is for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and the app asks for your date of birth before an account is created. Video chat, voice chat, the forum and uploading photographs are restricted to players aged 18 and over, and that restriction is enforced by the server rather than only by the app.

Video and voice

Nothing is recorded

Video and voice pass live between players through our video provider and are never stored — not by the app, not by us, not by the provider. There are no recordings, so there is nothing to hand over, leak or delete. What is kept is the fact that a session happened and how long it lasted, because gold bars are charged by the minute.

Your camera is off until you switch it on, and switching it off stops the picture immediately. Your microphone is used only while you are in a game or a voice room.

Advertising

The app shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. To do that, AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier and technical information about your device, and may use it to choose which advertisements you see. This is handled by Google, not by us — we never receive your advertising identifier, and we cannot tell which advertisements you were shown.

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in your phone's settings, under Google › Ads. Google explains what it collects at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Who else sees your data

Nothing about you is sold, and nothing is shared for anybody else's advertising. These are the services the app needs in order to work:

Information may also be disclosed where the law requires it, or where it is necessary to investigate fraud, abuse or a threat to someone's safety.

Reports and moderation

If you report another player, the report is kept so a moderator can act on it. A report made during a game includes a picture of the board at that moment, and may include a single frame of the video you are reporting. Reports are kept for up to 2 years. If you later delete your account, the reports you made are kept with your name removed from them, so decisions about other people remain reviewable.

Deleting your account

In the app, at any time

Open Profile, scroll to the bottom and choose Delete my account. Everything held about you is erased straight away — profile, wallet, photographs, friends, posts, game history and sign-in — and it cannot be undone. Receipts survive, with no name on them, because tax law requires records of money received to be kept. Full detail is on the account deletion page.

How long things are kept

  • Your account and everything in it: until you delete it.
  • Purchase records: as long as the law requires, normally 7 years, with no name attached once the account is gone.
  • Reports: 2 years.
  • Counts such as games played per day: indefinitely, because they contain nothing that identifies anyone.

Your rights

You can see what is held about you, correct it, or have it erased. Most of it you can do yourself inside the app — your profile, your pictures, your posts and your whole account. For anything else, write to lakshaykumaar@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and you will get a reply within 7 days.

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to object to processing, to ask for a copy of your data in a portable form, or to complain to a data protection authority. Players in India may raise a grievance with the developer at the address above.

Where your data is

The database and stored pictures are held on servers operated by Supabase, and live video and voice are carried by LiveKit's servers. Both may be located outside your country, so using Roll Adda means your data may be processed abroad.

Security

Connections between the app and our servers are encrypted. Access to your account is controlled by your Google sign-in, and one account can be signed in on one phone at a time. No system is perfect, and no promise is made here that could not be kept.

Changes to this page

If what the app does with your data changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top is updated. A change that materially affects you will be announced in the app rather than only here.
