Delete your Roll Adda account and data

App: Roll Adda: Video Board Games
Package: com.videoludo.game
Developer: GreatFace

You can delete your Roll Adda account at any time, and everything held about you goes with it. There are two ways to ask.

In the app

Open Profile, scroll to the bottom and choose Delete my account. You will be asked to confirm. The account and its data are removed straight away, and you are signed out.

By email

Write to lakshaykumaar@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject Delete my Roll Adda account. Tell us your in-game username. We reply within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days of your request.

What is deleted

All of the following is erased permanently and cannot be recovered:

  • Your profile: username, avatar, date of birth, and sign-in details
  • Your wallet: gold coins, gold bars, and the record of how they moved
  • Every photograph you uploaded, both the profile picture and the album
  • Your friends, friend requests, and anyone you blocked
  • Your posts and comments in the forum
  • Your game history, results, and statistics
  • Notification tokens, so your phone stops receiving anything from us
  • Records of voice and video sessions you took part in

What is kept, and for how long

WhatWhyHow long
Purchase records (amount, product, date) Tax and accounting law requires us to keep records of money received As long as the law requires, normally 7 years
Reports made about other players Kept so moderation decisions about other people remain reviewable; your name is removed from them 2 years
Anonymous counts, such as games played per day Contains nothing that identifies you Indefinitely

Nothing kept can be used to contact you or to identify you, apart from the purchase records, which the law obliges us to hold.

Things worth knowing before you delete

  • Gold coins and gold bars are cancelled and are not refundable.
  • Deletion cannot be undone. Signing up again creates a new, empty account.
  • Your username is released and someone else may take it.
  • If you are in the middle of a game, you leave it and forfeit the buy-in.

Video and voice

Roll Adda does not record video or voice. Calls pass between players live and are never stored, so there are no recordings to delete. What is kept is the fact that a session happened and how long it lasted, used to charge gold bars — and that is deleted with your account.
