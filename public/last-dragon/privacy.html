Privacy Policy

The Last Dragon · Last updated 31 August 2026

The Last Dragon is a game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own. Nothing you do in the game is sent to us, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

The one exception is advertising, which is handled by Google. This page explains what that means in practice.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things locally so it can pick up where you left off:

  • The name you give your dragon
  • The highest level you have reached
  • Your rankings list
  • Whether you have health waiting from a rewarded ad

All of it lives on your phone and never leaves it. Uninstalling the app deletes it permanently, and we have no copy.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Two kinds: full-screen ads at breaks between levels, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch in exchange for health. You are never required to watch one to keep playing.

To select and measure those ads, Google may collect:

  • Your advertising ID
  • Device information such as model, operating system version and language
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address — not precise or GPS location
  • Whether an ad was shown, viewed or interacted with

This collection is Google's, under their own privacy policy, and we receive none of it. We see only anonymous totals: how many ads were shown and what they earned. Nothing that identifies a person reaches us.

You can read how Google uses data from apps that use its services, and reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings → Google → Ads on your Android device. Doing so does not affect the game.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number
  • We do not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files
  • We do not request precise or GPS location
  • We do not sell or share data with anyone — we hold none to sell
  • We have no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of our own

Children

The Last Dragon is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, contact us and we will look into it — though in practice the app collects nothing that could identify a child.

Your choices

Where the law requires it, Google will ask for your consent to personalised advertising before any ad is shown, and you can change that choice later. You can also reset your advertising ID as described above. To remove everything the game has stored, uninstall it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. If a change is significant, we will say so in the app.
