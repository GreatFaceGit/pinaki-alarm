What you get

Custom alarm sounds

Choose from the full Pro sound library instead of the single default tone, and set a different sound for each alarm.

Dismiss challenges

Stop the alarm only after solving a math problem, scanning a QR code you've placed across the room, or shaking your phone. For people who switch alarms off in their sleep.

Gradual volume

Alarms fade in from quiet to full volume instead of starting at full blast.

Longer notes

Write up to 100 characters on each alarm instead of 10, so a reminder can actually say what it's for.

Avatar pack

Unlock a set of profile icons to use in place of your earned badge, visible to your friends in Nudge.

What stays free

Pinaki Alarm itself remains free, with no limits introduced by this upgrade. Setting alarms in local or UTC time, earning Charity Points, badges, and the entire Nudge feature — friends, groups, and sending alarms to other people — are all free and stay free. Pinaki Pro adds optional features; it does not remove or restrict anything.

How delivery works

Pinaki Pro is a digital product delivered electronically. After a successful payment, the upgrade is applied to the Pinaki Alarm account associated with the email address used at checkout, usually within a few minutes. Open the app while signed in to that account and the Pro features become available immediately. There is nothing physical to ship and no delivery charge.

Requirements

  • A Pinaki Alarm account (created free inside the app)
  • The upgrade applies to your account, not to a single device
