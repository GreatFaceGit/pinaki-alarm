What it does

⏰  Real alarms that ring through silent mode
🌍  Set alarms in your local time or in UTC
📝  Add a note to any alarm so you know what it's for
👥  Nudge — send an alarm to a friend or a whole group

Nudge

Add friends by username, make groups, and send someone an alarm with a note attached. They accept or deny it — once accepted, it rings on their phone at the right moment, converted to their own local time wherever they are.

Useful for raid times, study groups, gym partners, prayer times, and anyone in a different timezone to you. Nudge is completely free.
