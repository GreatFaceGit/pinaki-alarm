Delete your Video Ludo account

You can request permanent deletion of your Video Ludo account and all data associated with it at any time.

To request deletion, email lakshaykumaar@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account (if you signed in with Google), or include your in-app username if you played as a guest.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile — username, avatar, date of birth
  • Your wallet — coin and gold bar balance, transaction history
  • Your friends list and any pending friend requests
  • Your game history and statistics
  • Any reports you filed or were named in

What we keep, and why

We may retain minimal records of purchases already made, and of moderation actions such as bans, for as long as required for accounting, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. This data is kept separately from your identifiable profile once the account is deleted.

How long it takes

Deletion requests are processed manually and completed within 30 days. You'll receive a confirmation email once it's done.

Guest accounts

If you played as a guest and never signed in with Google, your account exists only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it — there is nothing further to request.

See also our Privacy Policy.
