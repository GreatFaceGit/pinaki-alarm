Help with local & UTC notifications

Setting a notification

On the main screen, tap the Local or UTC toggle in the "Set Notification" card, pick a time, and optionally add a short note (10 characters). Tap "Add Notification" to save it.

Local vs. UTC

Swipe the clock card, or tap the arrows beside it, to switch between your device's local time and UTC. Each notification you set belongs to whichever clock was selected when you created it — you'll see a LOCAL or UTC tag next to each one in your list.

Removing a notification

Find it in your notifications list and tap "Remove."
